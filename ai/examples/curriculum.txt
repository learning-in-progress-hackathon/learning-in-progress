BIO 101 - Introduction to Cell Biology
Undergraduate, 1st year - Fall semester (12 weeks)

Course goal
Understand the structure and function of cells, from molecules to whole-cell processes.

Weekly topics
Week 1  - What is life? Characteristics of living things, the cell theory
Week 2  - Chemistry of life: water, pH, carbon and macromolecules
Week 3  - Proteins: amino acids, folding, enzymes
Week 4  - Prokaryotic vs. eukaryotic cells, organelles overview
Week 5  - Cell membrane: structure, diffusion, osmosis, active transport
Week 6  - Midterm review + MIDTERM EXAM
Week 7  - Cellular respiration: glycolysis, Krebs cycle, electron transport chain
Week 8  - Photosynthesis: light reactions and the Calvin cycle
Week 9  - DNA structure and replication
Week 10 - Gene expression: transcription and translation
Week 11 - Cell cycle, mitosis and meiosis
Week 12 - Final review

Assessment
- Midterm exam (week 6) ........ 30%
- Lab reports (4 total) ........ 20%
- Final exam (week 13) ......... 50%

Final exam
Covers all weeks, with extra weight on weeks 7-11.
Format: 40 multiple-choice questions + 3 short-answer questions, 2 hours.
